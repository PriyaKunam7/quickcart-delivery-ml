# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity Levels
Incidents are classified as SEV1 through SEV4 based on customer
impact and scope.

### SEV1
Full outage affecting all customers. Page the on-call lead immediately.

### SEV2
Partial outage or major degradation affecting a subset of customers.

## Communication
All incidents must be logged in the incident tracker within 15 minutes
of detection, with regular status updates posted every 30 minutes
until resolution.
